Here is a quick breakdown of how it works:Active Apps & Files: When you open a program, a web browser tab, or a game, it is copied from your storage drive into the RAM.Real-Time Processing: The CPU constantly reads and writes to the RAM to process your keystrokes, clicks, and background tasks in real-time.Temporary Storage: RAM only holds data while the device is powered on. When you close an app or turn off your device, that space in the RAM is cleared out.
